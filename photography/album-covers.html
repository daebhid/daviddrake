David worked as an album cover photographer and artworker for over a decade, working with graphic designers like Samuel Burgess-Johnson and Paul Flack, creating hundreds of album covers for various artists and labels. In 2017, the album cover David shot for The 1975 was nominated for a Grammy for Best Recording Package.
