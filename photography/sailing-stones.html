Sailing Stones

  1. Year2015
  2. TypePhotography
  3. StatusCompleted

Sailing Stones is a series of medium format photographs shot in Death Valley, California in 2015. The series was published by Chloride City as a foldout A3 pamphlet alongside a short story.
