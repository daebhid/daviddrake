A Burning Cloud

  1. Year2015
  2. TypePhotography
  3. StatusCompleted

A Burning Cloud is a collection of 35mm, medium format, and digital photographs created a decade apart in Upstate New York. The series was self-published as a soft cover book and a zine. Both versions and subsequent reprints are now sold out.
