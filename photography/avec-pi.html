Avec Pi

Back to Photography

Avec Pi is a series of 35mm black and white photographs taken in 2016 across East Anglia and Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The series was self-published as a hardcover photobook under David's Chloride City imprint. A limited number of signed hardcover books are still available. Send an email to daviddrakeprojects@gmail.com if you'd like one (£15 plus shipping).

Exhibitions

  • Book Fair | Unveil'd Photo Fair, Exeter, 22-23 October 2016
  • Book Fair | On Paper Festival, Norwich, 3 December 2016
  • Group Show | Nunn's Yard, Norwich, Obscura Presents, 12 April 2018
