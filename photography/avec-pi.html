Avec Pi

  1. Year2016
  2. TypePhotography
  3. StatusCompleted

Avec Pi is a series of 35mm black and white photographs taken in 2016 across East Anglia and Nouvelle-Aquitaine. The series was self-published as a hardcover photobook under David's Chloride City imprint. A limited number of signed hardcover books are still available.
