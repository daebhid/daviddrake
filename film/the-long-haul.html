The Long Haul

Back to Film

The Long Haul is the debut feature film by writer and director David Drake. It stars Margo Martindale as CJ Montague, a long-haul truck driver in Idaho who is facing retirement and the potential paroling of a prisoner who destroyed her life 25 years earlier. It shot in New Mexico in 2025 and was produced by Braintrust and Spiral Stairs.

The Long Haul, 91 minutes, Official Selection, Tribeca Festival
