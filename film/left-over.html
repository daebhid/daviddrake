Left Over

  1. Year2022
  2. TypeShort Film
  3. StatusReleased

Left Over is a short film by writer and director David Drake. It stars Chloe Pirrie as Nell, who packs up her late father's home while unpacking memories of their volatile relationship, alongside Ian Hart as Mick. It was produced by Ludovico Zanette for Pylon, with support from the BFI Network Short Film Fund, and was selected as a Vimeo Staff Pick in 2022.
