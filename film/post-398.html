Post 398

  1. Year2020
  2. TypeShort Film
  3. StatusReleased

Post 398 is a 2020 short documentary directed and produced by David Drake. It follows a Harlem jazz room in the basement of a veterans association, as the band tries to keep the place going after the death of its founder. It was broadcast on Kweli TV.
