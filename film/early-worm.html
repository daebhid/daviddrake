Early Worm

  1. Year2019
  2. TypeShort Film
  3. StatusReleased

Early Worm is a short film written, directed, and produced by David Drake. It follows Charlie, who gets a job delivering leaflets in an attempt to straighten out his life, but a chance encounter with a childhood friend sends him down a self-destructive path. It was distributed by Shorts International for European broadcast.
