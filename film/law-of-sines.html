Law of Sines

Back to Film

Law of Sines is the story of a troubled outsider who drifts across Scotland in the late 1940s while working for the Ordnance Survey, all the while trying to evade a dark entity that stalks him wherever he goes. It is the second feature written by David Drake. The film is currently in casting.
