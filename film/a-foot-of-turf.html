A Foot of Turf

  1. Year2018
  2. TypeShort Film
  3. StatusReleased

A Foot of Turf is a short documentary directed by David Drake. A remote island's culture is fading away due to an economic downturn, but islanders both near and far return every week to watch their beloved soccer team.
