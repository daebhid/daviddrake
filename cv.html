David Drake is a professional screenwriter, director, photographer, and Prince2 accredited project manager.

The Long Haul (formerly known as Dead Letters) is a feature film written and directed by David Drake for Spiral Stairs Entertainment and Braintrust Productions. The film stars Margo Martindale, Steven Root, Cole Sprouse, Wes Studi, Yalitza Aparicio, and Jefferson White. There has been press published on Deadline, The Hollywood Reporter, and Variety about the film.

Law of Sines is a feature film written by David Drake for Braintrust Productions.

Blowdown is a feature film written by David Drake for Pylon Films.

Modern Washout is a feature film written by David Drake with support from the BFI Network Early Development Fund.

Left Over is a short film written and directed by David Drake with the support of the BFI Network Short Film Fund. The film stars Chloe Pirrie and Ian Hart. It was featured as a Vimeo Staff Pick.

Post 398 is a short film by David Drake for Donuts Films. The film has been broadcast on Kweli TV. It played at the Bushwick Film Festival, the Oxford International Short Film Festival, and the International Documentary Film Festival of Buenos Aires.

Early Worm is a short film written and directed by David Drake for Kamiokande Films. The film was distributed by Shorts International for broadcast on European television.

A Foot of Turf is a short film by David Drake for Kamiokande Films. The film won best short film at the Manlleu International Film Festival and was showcased online by NOWNESS.

Party Wall is a short film written and directed by David Drake. The film premiered at the Bucharest Short Film Festival and was showcased online by No Budge.

David has also directed dozens of music videos for artists like Kele Okereke, Live Footage, IDER, Aniseed, Alex Kozobolis, Amy Milner, Zach Said, and Olympians.

His photography work has been published widely across publications like Teen Vogue, Creative Review, Nero, and Clash.

He has photographed and designed over one hundred album covers for artists like The 1975, Glass Animals, Django Django, Tom Grennan, Haerts, etc. In 2017, the album cover he shot for The 1975 was nominated for a Grammy for Best Recording Package.

Avec Pi is a photographic work created by David Drake and published as a hardcover book by Chloride City.

Common Era is a photographic work created by David Drake. It was shown as a solo exhibition at the Norwich Arts Centre.

Carousel is a photographic work created by David Drake and self-published as a zine.

Sailing Stones is a photographic work and short story created by David Drake and published as a pamphlet by Chloride City.

A Burning Cloud is a photographic work created by David Drake and published as a hardcover book by Chloride City.

A Life Cliched is a short story written by David Drake for the PS Publishing hardcover anthology Postscripts 19: Enemy of the Good (2009).

Remedy Blue is a short story written by David Drake for Big Pulp's paperback anthology Big Pulp Annual (2016).

Short to Feature is an article written by David Drake for the Independent Cinema Office.

David has guest lectured at Norwich University of the Arts, Ravensbourne University London, and has spoken on various panels for the BFI Network. He served as a juror for Manlleu Film Festival in 2019.
