Photography

David is an American self-taught photographer based in Norwich, UK. His work spans personal projects, photobooks, exhibitions, and commissions. He has worked with artists including The 1975, Glass Animals, Tom Grennan, and Kele Okereke. His work has been published in magazines like Nero, Teen Vogue, Pages, and Clash. In 2017, the album cover he shot for The 1975 was nominated for an artwork Grammy.
