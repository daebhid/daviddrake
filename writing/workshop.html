Screenwriting Workshops

Back to Writing

David runs structured screenwriting workshops for writers who are looking to develop and share their work in a group setting. These are announced seasonally and held online.

How It Works

The workshops run in small groups of five to ten writers. The six sessions take place online over a three month period, giving the participants time to work independently in between meet ups. Each session combines group discussions, practical exercises, and feedback on the work.

What This Includes

  • Structured online sessions
  • Group discussion and feedback
  • Practical writing exercises
  • Story and character development
  • Dialogue and character work
