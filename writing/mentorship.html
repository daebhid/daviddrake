Mentorship

Back to Writing

David offers support to individual writers as they develop their craft through a structured six session mentorship programme. Regardless of whether it's a feature film or a short, writing a screenplay is a lot like getting lost in the woods (on purpose). This programme focuses on giving incremental guidance and encouragement to the writer as they navigate this process, from initial ideas to a finished script.

How It Works

The programme runs across six sessions, spread over six months to a year depending on the writer's pace, in person in Norwich or online. Each session builds on the last, moving from initial ideas through character and story development to the craft of writing the screenplay itself.

What This Includes

  • Nurturing ideas
  • Constructing characters
  • Unearthing a story
  • Crafting the screenplay
  • Structured six session plan

Let's Discuss Your Project

Whether you have an idea already or are starting from scratch, get in touch to discuss the programme. Because every writer is different, fees are agreed individually based on the scope of the engagement. To discuss in more depth, email David at daviddrakeprojects@gmail.com.
