Through his script consultancy work, David helps both individual writers and production companies develop and strengthen their screenplays. He works in-person across East Anglia and London, as well as online with international clients.

How It Works

There's no single solution to the puzzle of writing a screenplay. It requires persistence, vulnerability, focus, and a critical eye. David offers feedback and critique backed by his craft and experience to both individual writers and production companies seeking outside development support.

Engagements are bespoke and set by the client's needs, a one-off consultation on a single draft, or ongoing support across a full redraft process. Sessions can be arranged as a one-off, ongoing, or as-needed basis.

What This Can Include

  • Written feedback
  • Structural breakdowns
  • Line-by-line notes on the script
  • Character development
  • Dialogue polish
  • Organizing early ideas
  • Discussions over Zoom or in person

Let's Discuss Your Project

Whether it is a treatment, synopsis, feature screenplay, or short film, get in touch to discuss your project. Because every project is unique, fees are agreed individually based on the scope of the engagement. To discuss in more depth, email David at daviddrakeprojects@gmail.com.
